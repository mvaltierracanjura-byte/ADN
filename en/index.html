About us

A Mazatlán studio that works with AI and shows up in person.

ADN started by building the whole system for a local coffee shop: online ordering, the bar screen, payments, delivery pricing by neighborhood and a stamp card. We learned that small businesses don't need generic software with a hundred features. They need a few tools, done well, that respect how they already serve their customers.

  • We never make up your prices, hours or policies.
  • The AI handles the repetitive questions; anything it doesn't know goes to a person on your team.
  • Your customers always know they're talking to an assistant and can ask for a person.
  • Your domain, your database, your customers.
